The problem

Founders run the company in the gaps between email and meetings.

The inbox never ends

Replies to customers, investors and candidates wait on one person.

Meetings eat the day

Intro and qualification calls repeat the same questions, then need notes and follow-ups.

Follow-ups slip

CRM updates and promised next steps fall through the cracks.

The solution

Delegate the way you'd brief a great chief of staff.

“Reply to my unanswered emails from today.”

“Join my 2pm with Northwind — find out their budget and timeline.”

It does the work in your voice, asks before anything risky goes out, and posts what it did back into the same chat.

Hand-off 1 · Email

“Reply to my emails.”

  • Drafts each reply in your voice, with context from past threads
  • Holds anything risky for your approval
  • Reports back with the exact text it sent

Hand-off 2 · Meetings

“Join my 2pm and qualify them.”

  • Joins as a disclosed AI notetaker, or speaks in your cloned voice
  • Asks for consent before recording
  • Returns a summary, a lead score and a follow-up draft

Hand-off 3 · Your day

Everything it did today, on one page.

  • Meetings attended, emails sent, follow-ups queued
  • What still needs you, at the top
  • The same view on your phone, with push approvals

Trust is the product

You only delegate to something you can stop.

Approvals with hard floors

New recipients, money and commitments always ask first. Approve from web, email or a phone notification.

Stop it in one step

A kill switch in every app, or reply STOP to any email it sends. It never double-replies after you do.

Consent and disclosure

It discloses that it's AI, asks before recording, and won't clone a voice that doesn't match yours.
