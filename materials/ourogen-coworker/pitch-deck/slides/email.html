Hand-off 1 · Email

“Reply to my emails.”

  • Drafts each reply in your voice, with context from past threads
  • Holds anything risky for your approval
  • Reports back with the exact text it sent
From the live product: the user asks in chat, the coworker drafts replies, the user approves, and the coworker posts what it sent back into the same thread. You never wonder what it said on your behalf.
