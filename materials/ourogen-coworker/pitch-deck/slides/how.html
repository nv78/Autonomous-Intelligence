How it works

Four steps from sign-up to hand-off.

1

Connect

Gmail, Calendar, Slack and HubSpot, in a few clicks.

2

Learn

It reads 90 days of history to learn your people, deals and writing style.

3

Delegate

Ask in chat. It drafts, joins calls and follows up.

4

Approve

One tap on web, email or phone. Autonomy grows as trust does.

Onboarding takes minutes. The approval step is the product's core loop: every edit or rejection teaches it, and the track record (acceptance rate, edits, hours saved) is visible in Activity.
