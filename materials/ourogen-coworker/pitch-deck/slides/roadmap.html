Go-to-market and roadmap

Start with founders, then their teams.

Now

  • Founder pilot on Gmail or Outlook
  • Google and Microsoft app verification
  • Live vendor checks

Next

  • Role pages for sales, recruiting, investing
  • Auto-join chosen meetings
  • Streaming voice on calls

Later

  • Team plan with SSO
  • Org-wide governance
  • A coworker per employee

Built-in distribution: every call it joins and every email it sends introduces Ourogen to someone new.

Wedge: founders and small sales teams, where email and intro calls are the job. Expansion: Team plan, where an employer sets policies and each employee gets a coworker. Disclosure on calls doubles as a referral channel.
