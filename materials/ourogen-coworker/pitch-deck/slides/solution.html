The solution

Delegate the way you'd brief a great chief of staff.

“Reply to my unanswered emails from today.”

“Join my 2pm with Northwind — find out their budget and timeline.”

It does the work in your voice, asks before anything risky goes out, and posts what it did back into the same chat.

The interface is the chat people already know. No workflows to build. Each hand-off becomes a chat thread, so the left sidebar is a record of everything it has done.
