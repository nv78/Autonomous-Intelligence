# Your first 10 minutes

This page takes you from sign-up to a coworker that answers email for you and runs a task every morning.

## 1. Sign up and pick a workspace

Sign up at your Ourogen URL with Google, Microsoft, or email, then open **Coworker**. Onboarding offers two options:

- **Try the demo workspace**: a fictional inbox, Slack, calendar, and docs. Nothing real is read or sent. It's a safe way to see what the coworker does.
- **Connect your accounts**: Google (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) or Microsoft 365 (Outlook mail and calendar), plus Slack. Start with **read** access. You can grant **send** access later from **Settings**.

Connecting a real account replaces the demo workspace. The first sync reads recent mail, events, and messages, then builds your coworker's memory: people, companies, open threads, decisions, and how you write.

## 2. Give it its first job

In the chat, type:

> Reply to my unanswered emails

The coworker drafts one reply per waiting thread, in your voice. Each draft shows up as a card. **Approve** sends it, **Edit** lets you change it first, and **Reject** drops it. After it sends, the chat shows exactly what went out.

## 3. Hand off something for good

> From now on, handle inbound sales leads

This proposes a **responsibility**: a standing job with a trigger. Confirm the card, and every new lead gets a drafted reply waiting for your approval.

## 4. Put something on a schedule

> Every weekday at 8:30am, tell me what's on my plate today

This proposes a **scheduled task**. Confirm it, and each weekday morning a run lands in its own chat. See [Scheduled tasks](scheduled-tasks.md).

## 5. Reach it from where you are

- **Phone**: install the mobile app, or link your number in **Settings → Text messages** and text it.
- **Slack**: once your admin turns on the bot, @mention it in any channel or DM it.

See [Ways to reach your coworker](channels.md).

## 6. Decide how much it does on its own

Everything starts at **Ask first**. When you trust it with a kind of work, loosen it in **Settings → Autonomy**. See [Approvals and autonomy](approvals.md).
