# Meetings and your voice

## Send it to a call

> Join my 2pm with Dana and find out their timeline

The coworker joins through a meeting bot (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams). It says it's your AI, follows your instructions, and escalates pricing, contracts, or anything it's unsure about. When the call ends, it posts the summary, a lead score, and a drafted follow-up in the same chat.

## Take every external call

> Join all my external intro calls from now on

This creates the **Join my external meetings** responsibility.
- **Before each matching meeting**, you get a prep brief in its own chat, with a one-tap **Skip** (also sent by email and push).
- **At the start**, it joins.
- **Notes**: add instructions for the call by replying in that chat before it starts.

## Live control

While a call runs, **Coworker → Calls** (or the mobile app) shows the live transcript. You can whisper an instruction that only the coworker sees, or tap **Take over**: the coworker leaves, writes up the call so far, and opens the meeting link for you.

## Consent and recording

The bot announces itself in the meeting chat and by voice. In all-party-consent mode (**Settings → Calls**, or forced by your organization), it asks everyone before taking notes. See [Trust & safety](../trust.md).

## Your voice twin

With your verified consent (a recorded statement in your own voice), the coworker can speak in a clone of your voice. It's still introduced as your AI. Set it up under **Coworker → Twin**. You can delete the twin and every sample at any time.
